Flips and BRRRR

What a short-term loan does not cover comes from the same place as a rental's cash to close (home equity or cash, above). Flip profit is taxed as ordinary income; the tax % is a rough placeholder, so check it with a CPA.

Percentages of rent apply to collected rent. Your DSCR is net income ÷ mortgage; the lender's is rent ÷ mortgage, taxes, insurance and HOA. Reserves are months of that full payment. Funds available is cash plus the equity you can draw. With home equity, its payment comes out of each deal's cash flow. Changes re-score every deal.

Google Drive backup and sync

Your data is only on this device.

Sync keeps one file, dealdesk-backup.json, in your own Google Drive and merges it with this device. Run it on each device to keep them matched. DealDesk can only see the file it creates, nothing else in your Drive.

Property lookup

Lets the Add a deal form fill in a property from its address: units, bedrooms, size, year built, tax bill, last sale, an estimated value and an estimated rent. It uses RentCast, a property data service, with your own key. Get a free key at rentcast.io (API section); the free plan covers 50 requests a month and one lookup uses three. With a key saved, the values of properties you own also refresh once a month when you open the app, one request each, and each card has a Refresh value button.

The key is kept on this device only. It is not in your Drive backup or the export file, so paste it once on each device.

Backup and restore

No Google account needed. Export saves everything on this device to a file. Import merges a backup file into this device; nothing already here is lost unless the file has a newer version of it.
